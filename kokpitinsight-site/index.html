How it works

The language model never invents your business logic. It interprets the question; a governed semantic layer decides what the numbers mean.

01

Ask

Users ask in natural Turkish, with follow-ups like "only in TL" or "break it down by month".

02

Understand

Intent, entities, filters and time ranges are resolved against your company's own vocabulary.

03

Compile

Approved metric and dimension definitions are compiled into SQL that is deterministic and safe.

04

Answer

Results come back as tables, charts and written insight, with the SQL and lineage one click away.

Built for trust, not just demos

Text-to-SQL is easy to demo and hard to trust. We built the platform around one principle: when the system isn't sure, it asks instead of guessing.

Governed semantic layer

Metrics, dimensions, vocabulary and filters follow a Draft → Candidate → Released lifecycle, so every answer comes from an approved definition.

Deterministic compiler

A semantic AST compiles queries in a predictable way. The model chooses what to ask; it never writes free-form SQL against production data.

Automated discovery

AI agents explore new schemas and propose metrics and dimensions. People review the proposals in an inbox before anything goes live.

Measured continuously

Golden-question test suites and nightly retrieval-health checks catch regressions before users notice them.

Asks when unsure

Ambiguous entity, missing required filter, out-of-scope question: the assistant offers choices instead of returning a confident wrong number.

Enterprise ready

Designed for on-premise deployment, with corporate SSO, encrypted connections and full query traceability for audit.

Why Turkish, why now

Turkish is agglutinative: one root word can take dozens of suffixed forms. Generic BI copilots built for English often get Turkish business questions wrong, and enterprises in Turkey still route simple data questions through analysts and static reports.

  • Turkish-first language handling. Suffix-aware matching, local business vocabulary, Turkish number and currency formats.
  • Multi-currency aware. TL, USD and EUR series with up-to-date exchange-rate conversion built in.
  • Works on the data you already have. Connects to existing relational databases with no data migration.
  • Transparent by default. Every answer shows its SQL, period, filters and the definitions behind it.
